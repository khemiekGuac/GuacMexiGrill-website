FAQs

Quick
Answers.

Do you offer catering?
Yes — order catering directly online at all 56+ locations. No quotes, no forms. Just order online.
Where are your locations?
56+ locations across Canada — Ontario, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, PEI, and more. Use our locations page to find the one nearest you.
How do I give feedback about my visit?
Use the form above — our team personally reviews every message.
Do you have a rewards program?
Yes — the GUAC Cash Back card earns you cash back on every purchase. Ask in-store to sign up — it’s free and instant.
Are your ingredients halal?
Please contact your nearest location directly for the most up-to-date information on ingredient sourcing and dietary accommodations.
How do I get a gift card?
GUAC Gift Cards are available in-store at all locations in any denomination. They never expire and are redeemable at any GUAC location across Canada.
