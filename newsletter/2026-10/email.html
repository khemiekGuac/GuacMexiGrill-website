Operational Update

Product Freshness & Quality Standards

Make Less, More Often – Delivering Freshness, Reducing Waste, Elevating Guest Experience

Why This Matters: Freshness is one of the strongest drivers of guest satisfaction. Our commitment to Make Less, More Often ensures every product served meets Guac Mexi Grill’s quality promise. Smaller batch production protects freshness, improves appearance, and reduces unnecessary waste — all while supporting operational excellence.

October Priorities
1.Guacamole Standards
●Prepare guacamole in single batches.
●At opening, prepare enough for lunch and dinner business periods.
●After the dinner rush, prepare one fresh batch for the next business day.
●Monitor usage throughout the day and avoid overproduction.
●Discard immediately any product that is oxidizing or does not meet freshness, appearance, or food safety standards.
2.Salsa Standards
●Transfer all remaining salsa nightly into a clean, sanitized pan.
●Label and store according to food safety procedures.
●Follow FIFO (First In, First Out) — older product must be used before fresh product.
●Never place fresh salsa on top of existing product.
●Replace pans as needed to maintain presentation and sanitation.
3.Vegetable Preparation Standards

Prep vegetables in smaller quantities throughout the day.

●Only prepare what is needed to support anticipated business levels while maintaining freshness, color, and crispness.
●Rotate product using FIFO to ensure older product is used first.
●Store vegetables in clean, sanitized, properly labeled containers.
●Never mix fresh product with existing product unless FIFO procedures are followed.
●Replenish in small batches as needed.
●Discard any vegetables that no longer meet freshness, quality, or food safety standards.
4.Freshness Reminder

Vegetables are one of the first products guests see. Preparing smaller batches more frequently keeps them crisp, vibrant, and visually appealing, reinforcing the quality guests expect from Guac Mexi Grill.

5.Key Expectations
●Prioritize freshness over volume production.
●Maintain clean and sanitized containers, utensils, and storage areas.
●Follow FIFO procedures consistently.
●Report any product quality concerns to management immediately.

Remember: Guests notice freshness. Smaller batches prepared more often deliver a superior guest experience.

Our Commitment to Excellence

By embracing the Make Less, More Often standard, we protect product quality, reduce waste, and strengthen our brand promise. Freshness is not just a procedure; it is a daily discipline that sets our brand apart from others.
