FAQs

Loyalty
Questions.

Is the loyalty card free to join?
Yes, completely free. There is no cost to sign up and no minimum spend required to start earning points.
Do my points expire?
No. Your GUAC loyalty points never expire as long as your account remains active (at least one visit per 24 months).
Can I use my loyalty card at all locations?
Yes. Your card is valid at all 56 GUAC Mexi Grill locations nationwide.
How do I redeem my points?
Simply present your loyalty card at the register when paying. A team member will apply your points to eligible rewards.
Can I earn points on gift card purchases?
Points are earned on food and beverage purchases. Gift card purchases and reloads do not earn loyalty points.
What if I lose my card?
Visit any GUAC location with your registered email address and we can issue you a replacement card with your full balance intact.
