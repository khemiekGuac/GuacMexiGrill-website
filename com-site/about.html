The Beginning

Raised On
Bold Flavour.

We started with a simple question: why does bold Tex-Mex have to be complicated? We set out to build something different — a place where every bite tastes like it was made with intention, because it was.

From day one, we committed to making everything in-house. Our salsas, our guacamole, our marinades — made in-house every single day. We use fire to bring out depth. We don't cut corners.

Guac Mexi Grill has grown to 56+ locations across Canada, and we're now bringing that same craft to the United States through franchising.

Our Mission

To redefine fast-casual Tex-Mex with house-made preparation, thoughtfully sourced ingredients, and a premium dining experience.

Our Vision

To bring bold Tex-Mex taste and a premium atmosphere across North America.

Bold Flavour
We don't do mild. Every recipe is built for maximum flavour — layered, smoky, and crafted with care. If it doesn't taste bold, it doesn't make the menu.
House-Made
No shortcuts, no powder packets. Our guacamole, salsas, and sauces are crafted in-house every single day. That's the Guac promise.
Fire-Roasted
Real fire brings out real flavour. We roast our peppers, proteins, and ingredients over heat to build depth that you can taste in every bite.
