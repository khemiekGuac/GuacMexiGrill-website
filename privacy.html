Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026  ·  guacmexigrill.ca

Introduction

Thank you for accessing the guacmexigrill.ca website operated by Guac Mexi Grill. We respect your privacy and want to protect your personal information. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

Guac Mexi Grill's Privacy Policy complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act ("PIPEDA") and the Data Protection Act. We are committed to complying with our Privacy Guidelines and these Privacy Principles whenever we collect and use personal data.

What We Collect

Guac Mexi Grill only collects personal information for legitimate business activities. For example, we may collect information when you:

  • Participate in a contest or promotion
  • Sign up for our cash back incentive or email newsletter
  • Submit feedback or contact our corporate office
  • Place a catering or online order
  • Visit our website (non-identifying technical data such as browser type, device, and access time)

Before Guac Mexi Grill collects any personal information, the reasons for collecting it are explained to you.

How We Use Your Information

Guac Mexi Grill will only use your personal information with your knowledge and consent. We will not disclose your information to any agents or others acting on your behalf without your consent. Personal information that is no longer required for specific purposes will be securely destroyed.

Where Guac Mexi Grill shares your personal information with professional advisors, franchisees, or suppliers to manage business operations, any such entities are required to comply with Guac Mexi Grill's Privacy Guidelines.

Disclosure of Information

Guac Mexi Grill does not sell or disclose personal information outside of its system. However, we reserve the right to use or disclose any information as needed to:

  • Satisfy any law, regulation, or legal request
  • Protect our business interests
  • Co-operate with any law enforcement investigation
  • Address matters concerning public policy and safety

Your Right to Access

Guac Mexi Grill recognizes that you may have the right to access your personal information, including how it is used and how to change, correct, challenge, or amend it.

Any access request must be made in writing to:

We encourage any individual to contact our Information and Help Line if you have concerns about our Privacy Policies.

Security

We employ industry-standard procedures and policies to ensure the safety of the information we collect and retain, and to prevent unauthorized use. Although we take reasonable steps to safeguard your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page. Your continued use of the site following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page or in writing at the address above.
