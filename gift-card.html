FAQs

Gift Card
Questions.

Do GUAC gift cards expire?
No. Your gift card balance never expires. There are no dormancy fees or hidden charges — every dollar stays on the card until you spend it.
Can I check my gift card balance?
Yes. Visit any GUAC location and ask a team member to check your remaining balance. Balance inquiries are always free.
Where can I use my gift card?
Your GUAC gift card is accepted at all 56 GUAC Mexi Grill locations across Canada.
Can I reload my gift card?
Absolutely. You can add any amount to your existing card at any GUAC location. Just bring it in and ask a team member.
Can I use a gift card online or for delivery?
Gift card redemption at the point of sale is supported in-store. Contact your local GUAC for the latest online ordering options.
What if my card is lost or stolen?
Unfortunately, lost or stolen gift cards cannot be replaced without proof of purchase. Keep your receipt safe.
