GUAC Mexi Grill was built on a single conviction: bold Tex-Mex flavour and Canadian-built quality belong together.

Our Values ↓
The Beginning

Raised on
Bold Flavour.

We started with a simple question: why does bold, authentic Tex-Mex have to be complicated? We set out to build something different — a place where every bite tastes like it was made with intention, because it was.

From day one, we committed to making everything in-house. Our salsas, our guacamole, our marinades — made in-house every single day. We use fire to bring out depth. We don't cut corners. We believe Canadian-built means something.

Today, GUAC Mexi Grill has grown to 56+ locations across Canada, and we're just getting started.

What Goes In

100% grain-fed chicken. Canadian Triple-A beef, marinated overnight in house-made seasonings and charred to order. No artificial flavours, no artificial colours, no preservatives. Every item built from scratch, every day.

We don’t source for convenience.
We source for flavour.

The Space

Our restaurants are designed to match what’s on the tray — industrial, intentional, and built for a premium fast-casual experience. Bold food deserves a space that takes it seriously.

Bold Flavour
We don't do mild. Every recipe is built for maximum flavour — layered, smoky, and crafted with care. If it doesn't taste bold, it doesn't make the menu.
House-Made
No shortcuts, no powder packets. Our guacamole, salsas, and sauces are crafted in-house every single day. That's the GUAC promise.
Fire-Roasted
Real fire brings out real flavour. We roast our peppers, proteins, and ingredients over heat to build depth that you can taste in every bite.

Tex-Mex Soul, Canadian Born.
Bold flavour starts here.
